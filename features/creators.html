Creator Hub · Communities

Build your trading community.

The Creator Hub gives you a community page on TradeZoro: indicators that run on ZoroCharts, written courses, a members’ feed, events and Discord roles for your plans. Communities are run by independent creators.

Start your communityRead the guide

TradeZoro does not verify creators or their claims. Screens show an example community. Education only. Not financial advice.

Why it exists

One home for what you teach

The problem

  • Indicators, lessons and chat sit on three platforms that don’t talk to each other.
  • Pine code you share can be copied the day you post it.
  • Members join the chat but never find the course.

With Creator Hub

  • One community page with indicators, courses, feed, events and a leaderboard.
  • Protected indicators run on members’ charts without showing the source.
  • Plans decide who gets which indicator, course and Discord role.

How it works

Creator Hub, step by step

Five steps from an empty page to a running community.

  1. 1

    Start in three steps

    A name and web address, the look of your page, and your first plan. Up to three communities per account.

  2. 2

    Set up your page

    Cover, logo, an about section and an intro video, with Indicators, Courses, Feed, Events and Leaderboard tabs.

  3. 3

    Publish an indicator

    Paste Pine Script v5 or v6. It is compiled on the same engine members’ charts use, then delivered protected to the members you choose.

  4. 4

    Build a course

    Modules and lessons with video links, text, PDFs and quizzes. Release lessons over time or in order.

  5. 5

    Run the feed and events

    Members post and comment; you pin or remove. Schedule events with a meeting link only members see.

Questions

Before you start a community

Can I start free?

Yes. Free plans, invite links and member import work today. Each account can run up to 3 communities.

Who handles refunds?

You do. You are an independent creator, so you set a refund rule that shows on your page and you answer your members. The Creator Rules cover what you must tell them.

How is my indicator code protected?

It is hidden and traceable, not unbreakable. Members run it on their chart without seeing the source, and each delivered copy carries a hidden mark that can be traced to the account that loaded it. No protection is perfect, so only publish what you can live with being copied.

How do Discord roles work?

You connect your Discord server and choose a role for each plan. Members link their Discord, get the role, and lose it when their membership ends.

What can’t I sell or post?

No promised profits, income or win rates, no fake proof or testimonials, no copied or leaked material, and no trading bots or signals run through TradeZoro. Read the full Creator Rules before you start.

Does TradeZoro vouch for creators?

No. Every community is run by an independent creator, and TradeZoro does not verify creators or their claims. Members can report a community from its page.
