Mentis · Curriculum

Learn ICT and SMT, in order.

64 written chapters with chart diagrams take you from candles and structure to SMT divergence, plus specialist tracks on volume profile and prop firms. Each lesson has a checklist, and your progress saves to your account.

Open the curriculumRead the guide

Written lessons with diagrams, not video. Education only. Not financial advice.

Why it exists

A path, not a playlist

The problem

  • Free trading content jumps between topics with no order.
  • Order blocks and liquidity make little sense without the structure behind them.
  • Without a record, it is hard to know what you have actually covered.

With Curriculum

  • Three parts in order, Foundation, Intermediate and Advanced, each building on the last.
  • Written lessons with chart diagrams you can read at your own pace and come back to.
  • A checklist per lesson, and progress saved on every device.

How it works

Curriculum, step by step

Four steps from the chapter list to your first milestone.

  1. 1

    See the whole path

    All chapters on one page, with filters for Foundation, Intermediate, Advanced and the specialist tracks, and a bar to resume where you stopped.

  2. 2

    Read the chapter

    Each chapter is a written lesson with chart diagrams, the chapter list down the side and a tick for each one you finish.

  3. 3

    Go deeper with a track

    Volume Profile and Order Flow (12 chapters) and Prop Firm (10 chapters), after the core path.

  4. 4

    Collect the milestones

    Badges for your first chapter, Foundations complete, SMT Certified and Curriculum complete.

Questions

Before you start chapter one

Is the curriculum free?

Yes. All 64 chapters, including both specialist tracks, are free with an account. No card required.

Do I have to go in order?

The chapters are built to be taken in order, because each one assumes the ones before it. You can open any chapter from the list.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Every chapter you complete is saved to your account and shows on your dashboard on any device.

Is it video?

No. The chapters are written lessons with chart diagrams.

Is this financial advice?

No. The curriculum teaches methods many traders use, for study. It does not tell you what to trade.
