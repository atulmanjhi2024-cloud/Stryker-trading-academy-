Global Monitor · World events board

Headlines and markets, on one screen.

Global Monitor puts an events map, a headline wire, cross-asset markets, a risk board and a written brief in one terminal. Open it before a session to see what is moving and where.

Open Global MonitorRead the guide

For context only. Each panel names its source inside the tool. Screens are the real tool on sample data. Education only. Not financial advice.

Why it exists

The outside world, without twenty tabs

The problem

  • Headlines, quotes and the economic calendar live in separate tabs.
  • On a busy news day it is hard to tell one story from ten.
  • A scheduled release can catch you in the middle of a session.

With Global Monitor

  • Markets, map, wire, risk and a brief, one tab each in one window.
  • Events on a world map, ranked by place, with a severity count on the wire.
  • A market calendar with impact colours beside the financial newswire.

How it works

Global Monitor, step by step

Five tabs, read in the order most members check them.

  1. 1

    Start with the markets

    Risk tone, breadth, rotation, curve and volatility on top; indices, US futures, havens, forex, energy, defence stocks and crypto below, each with a sparkline.

  2. 2

    See where events are happening

    Events on a world map coloured by type: armed conflict, military moves, civil unrest, diplomacy and sanctions, with the most active places and a tension index beside it.

  3. 3

    Scan the wire

    Headlines latest first, tagged by country and severity. Search them, or filter to critical, high or elevated.

  4. 4

    Check the risk board

    A readiness-level estimate and disease outbreaks ranked by severity, with prediction-market odds on geopolitical outcomes.

  5. 5

    Read the brief

    A rule-based written summary: the executive summary, the hot spots, a market read and the priority signals.

Questions

Before you open Global Monitor

What is it for?

It is the one-screen context check before a session: events, headlines, markets, risk and the calendar in one place.

Where does the data come from?

Public news, market and event sources. Each panel inside the tool names its source. For context only.

Is the brief written by AI?

No. It is a rule-based summary built from the same events and market data as the other tabs.

Where is it?

In the Terminal section of your dashboard, beside ZoroCharts and the Smart Money desk.

Which plan includes it?

Global Monitor is free for every account.

Is it trade advice?

No. It shows what is happening in the world, not what to trade. Education only. Not financial advice.
