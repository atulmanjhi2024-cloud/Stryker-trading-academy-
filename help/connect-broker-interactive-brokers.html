Connect Interactive Brokers: step by step

Beta · Last updated 21 November 2026

This guide connects Interactive Brokers to TradeZoro. Read the Connect your broker guide first if you want the big picture.

What you need
  • your Interactive Brokers (IBKR) account. IBKR connects with a Flex token that you create yourself in IBKR Client Portal. It is not a normal login box.
  • The accounts you want to share, ready at Interactive Brokers.
  • Pop-ups allowed for TradeZoro (the sign-in opens in a new tab).

Accounts that work

  • Live and paper IBKR accounts: the connection works from your IBKR account details. Our code does not tell live from paper, so check the account name shown in the list.
  • Brokerage accounts with stocks, options, futures, forex or crypto. The connection service lists these product types for IBKR.

Two-step login (2FA). If your IBKR account asks you to approve a login in the IBKR app when you open Client Portal, do that first. Which step asks for it is IBKR’s choice, not ours, and we have not tested every case.

Step by step

  1. Charts
  2. Trade menu
  3. Connect a broker
  4. Pick your broker
  5. Log in
  6. Choose accounts
  7. Done
  1. Open a chart and click Trade, then Connect a broker at the bottom of the menu. (Journal: Broker sync (beta).)
  2. In the connect window click Connect a broker. A new tab opens.
  3. Open the connect window and pick Interactive Brokers.
  4. The window asks for a Flex token. You create it in IBKR Client Portal (the window points you to IBKR’s own steps; IBKR’s menu names change, so follow IBKR’s page). Paste the token in the window.
  5. Choose the accounts to share and finish.
  6. Go back to the TradeZoro tab and click Continue. Your Interactive Brokers accounts show in the Trade menu.
  7. Journal only: pick the account and dates, click Load trades, check the preview, then Import.

What syncs

The connection service lists IBKR as data only. You get your accounts, balances, positions, orders and fills to read. You cannot place or change IBKR orders from TradeZoro.

Known limits

  • No order placing for IBKR. It is read only.
  • A Flex token can expire or be removed in IBKR. Then the connection stops and you connect again.
  • Balance is the figure IBKR last reported, not a live tick.

Disconnect

Trade menu, gear on the account, Disconnect, confirm. Imported journal trades stay. You can also remove access in Interactive Brokers’s own settings.

FAQ

I cannot find where to make a Flex token.

Open IBKR Client Portal and look for the reporting or Flex area. IBKR moves menus around. The connect window links to IBKR’s own steps.

Does TradeZoro see my IBKR password?

No. You never type your IBKR password into TradeZoro. The token only gives read access to reports.

Can I trade IBKR from the chart?

No. IBKR is read only here.

Something went wrong. Where do I look?

See Troubleshooting in Connect your broker. If you get “no clear answer from the broker”, check your working orders at Interactive Brokers before you send anything again.
