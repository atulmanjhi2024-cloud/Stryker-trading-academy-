Connect MetaTrader 5: step by step

Beta · Last updated 21 November 2026

This guide connects MetaTrader 5 to TradeZoro. Read the Connect your broker guide first if you want the big picture.

What you need
  • MetaTrader 5 on a computer, logged in to your trading account. Prop firm accounts log in with the MT login, password and server the firm gave you.
  • The accounts you want to share, ready at MetaTrader 5.
  • Pop-ups allowed for TradeZoro (the sign-in opens in a new tab).

Accounts that work

  • MetaTrader 5 accounts at a broker or prop firm: live, demo, evaluation and funded, logged in the same way as in MetaTrader.
  • Prop firm accounts (FundingPips and other firms that give you a MetaTrader login): log in to MetaTrader 5 with the login number, password and server name from the firm’s dashboard or welcome email. That is the only login you need. The firm’s website login is not used.
  • Forex, indices, gold, oil and the other CFDs your account can trade, sized in lots.
  • Each MetaTrader account is one connection. With two prop accounts you connect twice, each with its own terminal running.
Keep %(o)s running. MetaTrader has no cloud login for us to use, so the connector add-on in your terminal is the link. When the terminal is closed, the computer sleeps, or %(algo)s is off, TradeZoro cannot read the account or send orders. An order sent while the terminal is offline waits up to 90 seconds and then fails; it is not kept for later. An always-on computer or a Windows VPS keeps it running.

Prop firm rules. Some firms limit EAs or third-party tools on their accounts. The connector does not trade on its own, it only carries what you send from TradeZoro, but your firm decides what is allowed. Read your firm’s rules or ask its support before you connect.

Step by step

  1. Charts
  2. Trade menu
  3. Connect a broker
  4. Pick your broker
  5. Log in
  6. Choose accounts
  7. Done
  1. Open a chart and click Trade, then Connect a broker at the bottom of the menu. (Journal: Broker sync (beta).)
  2. In the connect window click Connect a broker. A new tab opens.
  3. Pick MetaTrader 5 in the list and finish the steps there.
  4. It shows connector details (an address, a connector ID and a connector secret). The secret is shown once: keep that tab open until you have pasted it.
  5. Download the connector add-on (an Expert Advisor, “EA”) from the link there. In MetaTrader 5 choose File › Open Data Folder and copy it into MQL5/Experts. Restart MT5 (or refresh Navigator › Expert Advisors).
  6. In Tools › Options › Expert Advisors tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL and add the address (only the https:// part and the host name).
  7. Drag the EA onto any chart. In its Inputs paste the address, connector ID, secret and secret version. Leave sign envelopes switched on.
  8. Switch on Algo Trading in the MT5 toolbar. After about 15 seconds the connector shows as online. Choose the account and finish.
  9. Go back to the TradeZoro tab and click Continue. Your MetaTrader 5 accounts show in the Trade menu.
  10. Journal only: pick the account and dates, click Load trades, check the preview, then Import.

Symbols: chart vs broker

MetaTrader brokers name their instruments their own way. The Nasdaq 100 is NDX100 at one firm and US100, NAS100, USTEC or US100.cash at others. TradeZoro charts use exchange symbols (NQ, ES, the index itself, or the forex pair).

  • When a MT5 account is picked, the order panel shows a MT5 symbol box next to the chart name. If your account’s symbol list says which one it is, it is filled in for you.
  • If not, pick it once from the list, or choose Other… and type the name exactly as MetaTrader’s Market Watch shows it (including any ending like .cash or .r). TradeZoro remembers it for that chart instrument and that account on this device.
  • One choice covers related charts: NQ, MNQ and Nasdaq 100 index charts all trade the Nasdaq 100 symbol you picked.

Prices differ. A futures chart (NQ) is not your broker’s CFD price (NDX100): the two move together but are not the same number. So from a futures or index chart only market orders go to MetaTrader, and no order lines are drawn on the chart. Limit and stop orders from the chart need a chart of the broker’s own instrument, such as a forex pair or spot gold (XAUUSD).

Size in lots

  • MetaTrader trades in lots, not contracts. The size box shows lots and steps by the account’s lot step (usually 0.01, the smallest lot).
  • What one lot is worth is set by your broker for each symbol. Check the symbol’s Specification in Market Watch before you size a trade.
  • TradeZoro sets no size limit of its own. Your broker applies its own lot limits and its message is shown if it refuses an order.

What syncs

Accounts, balance, open positions and working orders, read through the EA. Orders from TradeZoro (market, limit, stop) go to the EA, which places them in your terminal, and MetaTrader gives each one a ticket number. In TradeZoro, orders work after you enable live trading once.

Known limits

  • Your terminal must be running with the EA attached and Algo Trading on.
  • Stop loss and take profit: set them in MetaTrader for now. TradeZoro does not attach them to MetaTrader orders yet.
  • Closing a position: close it in MetaTrader for now. Working orders can be cancelled from TradeZoro.
  • No order lines or DOM ladder on a chart whose prices are not the broker’s instrument (see Symbols).
  • If you make a new connector secret, paste it into the EA, or connect again.

Disconnect

Trade menu, gear on the account, Disconnect, confirm. Imported journal trades stay. You can also remove access in MetaTrader 5’s own settings.

FAQ

I trade the NDX100 CFD on MT5 with a FundingPips account. How do I connect it?

Log in to MetaTrader 5 with the FundingPips MT login, password and server. Then follow the steps above: connect window, MetaTrader 5, copy the connector details, put the EA on a chart, paste the details, switch on Algo Trading. On an NQ or Nasdaq 100 chart pick NDX100 as the MT5 symbol once, and size in lots.

Does TradeZoro see my MetaTrader password?

No. You type it into MetaTrader only. TradeZoro and the connect window never ask for it. The EA uses its own connector secret.

The connector does not show online.

Check that the terminal is open, the EA is on a chart, Algo Trading is on, and the WebRequest list has the address from the connect window. If you made a new secret, paste the new one.

MetaTrader 4 or 5?

Use the one your broker or firm gave you a login for. The steps are the same; MT4 uses MQL4/Experts, MT5 uses MQL5/Experts.

My symbol is not in the list.

Choose Other… and type it exactly as Market Watch shows it. If the name is wrong the broker rejects the order; pick the box again to change it.

Can I use a phone?

The EA needs desktop MetaTrader; the phone app cannot run it. Once the desktop terminal is running, you can trade from TradeZoro on any device.

Something went wrong. Where do I look?

See Troubleshooting in Connect your broker. If you get “no clear answer from the broker”, check your working orders at MetaTrader 5 before you send anything again.
