Connect Tradovate: step by step

Beta · Last updated 21 November 2026

This guide connects Tradovate to TradeZoro. Read the Connect your broker guide first if you want the big picture.

What you need
  • your Tradovate login. You sign in on Tradovate’s own page and approve access (an OAuth sign-in).
  • The accounts you want to share, ready at Tradovate.
  • Pop-ups allowed for TradeZoro (the sign-in opens in a new tab).

Accounts that work

  • Tradovate live accounts and sim (demo) accounts on your login.
  • Prop firm accounts that run on Tradovate (they show as accounts under your Tradovate login). Many firms use a Tradovate login; if yours gave you a Rithmic login instead, use the Rithmic guide.
  • Futures, options and forex, as listed for Tradovate.

Account names. Tradovate also has a long number for each account. TradeZoro shows the account name from Tradovate. In the Trade menu you can rename an account with Edit details (label, prop firm and type: Evaluation, Funded, Live or Sim). Labels are for you only.

Step by step

  1. Charts
  2. Trade menu
  3. Connect a broker
  4. Pick your broker
  5. Log in
  6. Choose accounts
  7. Done
  1. Open a chart and click Trade, then Connect a broker at the bottom of the menu. (Journal: Broker sync (beta).)
  2. In the connect window click Connect a broker. A new tab opens.
  3. Open the connect window and pick Tradovate.
  4. A Tradovate sign-in page opens. Log in with your Tradovate username and password there.
  5. Approve access when Tradovate asks, then choose the accounts to share.
  6. Go back to the TradeZoro tab and click Continue. Your Tradovate accounts show in the Trade menu.
  7. Journal only: pick the account and dates, click Load trades, check the preview, then Import.

What syncs

Accounts, balance, positions, orders and fills. You can also place live orders from the chart once you enable live trading for your account.

Known limits

  • If you have a live and a sim account, both are listed. Check the name before you use one.
  • Balance is the last figure Tradovate reported plus fills since then, so it can lag a little.

Disconnect

Trade menu, gear on the account, Disconnect, confirm. Imported journal trades stay. You can also remove access in Tradovate’s own settings.

FAQ

Do prop firm accounts show up?

If the firm’s accounts sit under your Tradovate login, yes. Rename them with Edit details.

Same login as the Tradovate app?

Yes, the one you use at Tradovate’s own site.

Something went wrong. Where do I look?

See Troubleshooting in Connect your broker. If you get “no clear answer from the broker”, check your working orders at Tradovate before you send anything again.
