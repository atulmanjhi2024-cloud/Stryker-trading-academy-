Connect your broker

Beta · Last updated 21 November 2026

Connecting a broker links your trading account to TradeZoro. It is optional. This page explains what it does, what you need, and how to fix the common problems. Then pick your broker below for its own step by step guide.

In short
  • Your broker password stays with your broker. You log in on the broker’s own page, not on a TradeZoro form.
  • It reads your account. Trades and fills for the journal, open positions, and your balance.
  • Live orders after one step. Live trading is off until you enable it once for your account and accept the risk notice. Until then your broker accounts are view-only.

What connecting does

PartWhat you get
AccountsThe accounts you tick in the connect window appear in the Trade menu and in the journal sync.
Journal syncYour fills become journal trades (round trips from flat to flat). Syncing the same days twice adds nothing twice. Trades still open are not imported.
PositionsYour open position and working orders are read from the broker and shown on the chart panel.
BalanceThe broker’s last reported balance. If the broker’s figure is old, TradeZoro adds the profit or loss of fills made after it.
Live ordersFrom the chart, after you enable live trading once (see below). Small limits apply: NQ, ES, MNQ and MES, at most 2 contracts, 10 orders a minute. Interactive Brokers is read only.

Before you start

  • An account at the broker with its login working. Log in once at the broker yourself first.
  • A computer or tablet browser is easiest. The broker page opens in a new tab, so allow pop-ups for TradeZoro.
  • Your two-step login (2FA) device, if the broker uses one.
  • For a prop firm account: the login type your firm gave you (Rithmic, Tradovate or NinjaTrader). The firm’s website login is not the one to use.

How to connect

  1. Charts
  2. Trade menu
  3. Connect a broker
  4. Pick your broker
  5. Log in
  6. Choose accounts
  7. Done
  1. Open a chart and click Trade in the toolbar. (In the order panel you can also open the account picker and choose Connect a broker….)
  2. At the bottom of the menu click Connect a broker. The connect window opens.
  3. Click Connect a broker in the window. A new tab opens with the broker list.
  4. Pick your broker and log in there. Choose the accounts you want to share.
  5. Come back to TradeZoro and click Continue. The window closes and your accounts show in the Trade menu.

In the journal the same window is the Broker sync (beta) button. After connecting you choose an account and dates, click Load trades, check the preview and import.

Enable live trading

Live orders are real and use your own money at your broker. Before your first live order TradeZoro asks you once:

  1. In the Trade menu pick one of your broker accounts (Paper stays the default).
  2. Try your first order, or click Enable live trading. A short risk notice opens.
  3. Read it, tick the box and click Enable live trading. We save when you accepted it.

After that, clicking Buy, Sell, Flatten or the order book sends a real order to your broker. There is no extra confirm step. TradeZoro is not a broker and gives no advice; you are responsible for your orders. If you click Not now, the account stays view-only. TradeZoro can switch live trading off for everyone or for one account at any time.

How to disconnect

  1. Open the Trade menu.
  2. Click the small gear on the account, then Disconnect (or Manage to see all accounts), then confirm.

This removes the link. Trades already imported stay in your journal. You can connect again any time. You can also remove TradeZoro’s access in your broker’s own settings.

Privacy

  • We never see your broker password. You log in on the broker’s own page. For Rithmic, which uses a username and password, you type them in the secure connect window, and TradeZoro does not receive or store them.
  • TradeZoro reads your accounts, balance, positions, orders and fills. It sends an order only when you click to trade, after you enabled live trading.
  • Your account details stay in your own view. We do not show them to other members.

Troubleshooting

  • It asks me to log in again. The saved link expired. Open the Trade menu and click Reconnect. If that does not help, click Connect a broker and sign in again.
  • My account is not in the list. You did not tick it on the choose-accounts step, or the login you used cannot see it. Click Connect a broker again, sign in, tick the account. Brand new accounts can take a few minutes to appear.
  • The new tab did not open. Allow pop-ups for TradeZoro and click Open the broker tab again.
  • The balance looks old. Brokers refresh their balance on their own schedule. TradeZoro shows the broker’s last figure plus the result of fills after it, and re-reads it every 30 seconds while the Trade menu is open. Compare with your broker’s own screen.
  • “No clear answer from the broker.” The broker did not confirm your last order, so it may or may not be live. Check your working orders and position at the broker first. Do not send it again until you know. The chart blocks new orders until it can read the account again.
  • “Broker data is unavailable right now.” The connection service or the broker is down for a moment. Wait a minute and try again.
  • Journal import found no trades. Widen the dates. Only closed trades are imported.
