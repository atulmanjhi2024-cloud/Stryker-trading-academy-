Copy trading across your accounts

Beta · Last updated 21 November 2026

Copy trading sends an order you place on one of your accounts (the leader) to your other accounts (the followers). It works across brokers and with Paper. It only ever uses your own connected accounts. You cannot copy another member, and nobody can copy you.

Copied orders are real orders
  • Every live follower gets a real order at its broker, using that account’s money.
  • Prop firms and brokers may have rules about copy trading or trading the same idea on several accounts. Check your firm’s rules before you turn it on.
  • TradeZoro is not a broker and gives no advice. Copied orders are your own orders; see the Terms.

What is copied

  • Orders you place in TradeZoro on the leader: market, limit and stop orders, the stop loss and take profit you add, moving an order, cancelling it, and Flatten.
  • Each follower gets its order right after the leader’s order is accepted. If the leader’s order is refused or has no clear answer, nothing is sent to the followers.
  • Orders you place in your broker’s own platform are not copied. The broker connection does not give TradeZoro a reliable live feed of those orders, so we do not try.

Set it up

  1. Charts
  2. Trade menu
  3. Copy trading
  4. Leader
  5. Followers
  6. Save
  1. Connect your brokers and pick a live account once in the Trade menu, then Enable live trading (see Connect your broker).
  2. Open a chart, click Trade, then Copy trading at the bottom of the menu.
  3. Pick the Leader account.
  4. Switch on each follower you want and set its rules (below).
  5. Switch Copy trading on and click Save. The first time, a short confirm asks you to accept that copied orders are real orders.

Rules per follower

SettingWhat it does
On / offCopy to this account or not.
Same sizeThe follower gets the same quantity as the leader.
Multiplierx0.1 to x10 of the leader’s quantity, rounded to whole contracts. If it rounds to 0, that order is skipped for this account.
Fixed qtyThe same quantity on every copied order, whatever the leader trades.
ContractSame contract, or map it: Micro (NQ to MNQ, ES to MES) or Full size (MNQ to NQ). Same month.
Copy exitsOn by default. Copies Flatten and the stop loss / take profit. Off: only entries are copied, and you manage exits on that account yourself.

See what happened

After each copied order you see a short message per account, and the Copy status list in the panel shows every account: Sent, Filled, Rejected (with the broker’s message), or Skipped (with the reason). One account failing never stops the others. Every copied order is logged on its own, with its own duplicate guard, so a repeated tap never sends it twice.

Stop it fast

  • Pause copying: one tap. Orders on the leader still go; nothing is copied until you resume.
  • Flatten all copied: closes the chart’s contract (or its mapped contract) and cancels its working orders on every follower. The leader is not touched.
  • If TradeZoro switches live trading off for everyone or for your account, no copied order goes out either.

FAQ

Can I copy a friend’s trades, or let others copy mine?

No. Copy trading only works between accounts connected to your own TradeZoro login.

Can a follower be on a different broker?

Yes, any connected broker that allows orders from TradeZoro, plus Paper. Some brokers are read only (see each broker’s guide); copies to them are rejected with the broker’s message.

The leader filled but a follower was rejected. What now?

Read the message in Copy status, then check that account at its broker. TradeZoro does not retry on its own.
