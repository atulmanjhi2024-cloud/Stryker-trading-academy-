Chaikin Money Flow

TZTradeZoro VolumeFree v1.0 · Updated Nov 4, 2026
Add to chart

Chaikin Money Flow: 20-bar money flow volume divided by 20-bar volume, around a zero line.

Overview

Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) shows whether money has been flowing in or out over a window, in its own pane. Above zero means more closes near highs on volume; below zero means more near lows.

The line and fill are green above zero and red below.

How it works

Money flow volume = ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low) x volume. CMF = sum of money flow volume over 20 bars / sum of volume over 20 bars.

Values range from -1 to +1. Bars that close near their high add most of their volume; bars that close near the low subtract it.

How to use it

  • Some traders watch CMF staying above zero as steady buying pressure.
  • Use zero-line crosses as changes in flow.
  • Look for divergence between CMF and price.
  • Use it to check breakouts for volume support.

These are ways some traders read it, for learning. They are not trade signals.

Alerts

You can alert on its line crossing a value or the candle close (from the chart's + menu); checked while ZoroCharts is open.

Notes and limits

  • It needs volume data; on symbols without volume it shows nothing useful.
  • Bars with no range add nothing.
