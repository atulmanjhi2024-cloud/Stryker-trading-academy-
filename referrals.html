Questions

What counts as a verified invite?

A new account that signed up with your link, confirmed its email and used TradeZoro on 2 different days in its first 7. One reward per new account. Throwaway email addresses don't count.

Is there a limit?

Yes. You can earn up to 365 Pro days a year from invites. Your friend still gets their 5 days.

Does it stack with a paid plan?

Yes. If you already pay for Pro, the days you earn are saved on your account. Your billing is never changed.

Can I invite myself?

No. A second account, or a sign-up from the same device or network as yours, doesn't count.

When do the days arrive?

We check new invites once a day, so days land within 2 days of your friend meeting the rules. Nobody has to approve it. On the free plan, Pro switches on straight away.
